# TechNews ⚡

> Your Personal Tech Career Intelligence Platform

A personalized technology and career platform for developers and CS students.

TechNews brings technology news, competitive programming, hackathons, courses, jobs and career resources into one place.

Coming soon.

## ✨ Features

- 📰 Personalized technology news feed
- 🔎 Search news articles
- 🏷️ Category-based news filtering
- 📡 Multi-source news aggregation
- 🧠 Technology relevance filtering
- 📊 Article relevance and freshness ranking
- 🔖 Bookmark and save content
- 🏆 Competitive programming contests
- 🎓 Career and learning resources
- 🔐 Firebase Authentication
- 👤 Personalized user profiles

## 📰 News System

TechNews currently aggregates technology content from:

- NewsData.io
- Hacker News
- TechCrunch
- Ars Technica
- The Verge

The news pipeline includes:

1. Multi-source aggregation
2. Technology relevance filtering
3. Irrelevant-content filtering
4. Multi-layer content validation
5. Article deduplication
6. Relevance ranking
7. Freshness scoring
8. User-interest personalization
9. Search
10. Category filtering
